## Title

Isosyll: Always Listening, Barely Computing — Bit-Exact Streaming Speech Recognition on One CPU Core

## Authors (byline order)

Jepson Taylor; Ricky Ray Butler; Jordan Morrow; Alton Alexander; Jordan Hildebrandt; Besterlyne Jirongo

## Abstract (plain text, 799 of 1920 characters)

An always-on speech recogniser must keep its CPU cost low while audio arrives. Isosyll runs a pure-Rust streaming transducer with 60 MB INT8 artifacts for live and utterance-end final text. On one pinned quiet Zen 2 core, Isosyll live (160 ms, +LM) reaches 28.5x real time, 4.58% word error rate (WER), and 0.366 J per audio-second: 2.04x the throughput of Zipformer INT8 at 63% of its idle-subtracted core energy in paired full-panel blocks. Isosyll final reaches 4.24% WER (2.63% dev-clean) at 14.8x real time. On the unselected DEV half, final and live WER are -0.93 pp and -0.46 pp from Zipformer's. Live reranks close endpoint beams (+7.5% CPU, +9.4% energy, +329 MB RAM) both modes meet their registered edit gates (live 662 vs. 722 edits). All results are exploratory and use LibriSpeech DEV.
