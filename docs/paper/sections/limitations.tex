\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

\para{Exploratory measurements.} Every number in this draft comes from shared hosts outside a
qualifying window. A same-day A/A replay on such a host varied by up to \AASpread\%; the density
ladder used 20\,s rungs and a single repeat rather than the frozen three 120\,s repeats with
300\,s confirmations; and the CPU governor was not controlled. The qualified run on a quiet host
of the same CPU model may move every ratio, in either direction; the macros in this paper will be
regenerated from it unchanged.

\para{Accuracy.} \ultra is not competitive with the systems it outruns. Zipformer reaches
\WerRzeroDevClean\% on dev-clean and Nemotron \WerNemoDevClean\%; our best dense configuration
reaches \WerDtwoBestClean\%. The density comparison therefore compares systems at different
operating points. It shows what an integer-exact engine and a multirate encoder buy per core; it
does not show a better accuracy--cost trade-off than Zipformer at equal accuracy, and we do not
claim one. Training was unfinished when this draft was written, uses LibriSpeech only, and has
seen fewer epochs than the Zipformer recipe.

\para{Test sets.} LibriSpeech test-clean and test-other, and the sealed VoxPopuli and AMI sets,
are unread for this paper; all results are DEV. LibriSpeech test sets were read several times
earlier in the project, before the protocol was frozen; we disclose this, and no selection used
them.

\para{Scope.} Density is measured on one CPU family (Zen~2, AVX2, no VNNI or AVX-512) and one
ARM part; we make no claim about other microarchitectures. Nemotron has no path in the density
harness and so has no density row and no implied ceiling. Beam search improves WER but all speed
rows are greedy; its cost will be a separate, declared operating point. Latency is reported only
as lateness under load; word-level emission delay against forced alignments~\cite{mcauliffe2017mfa}
is part of the qualified run.
