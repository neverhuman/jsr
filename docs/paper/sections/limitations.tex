\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

\para{Where \ultra loses.} \ColdAccuracySentence{} Its speed is therefore compared across
operating points, never at equal accuracy. It is trained on LibriSpeech alone and its training is
unfinished: our Fig.~\ref{fig:money} point is \ColdHeadDetail. An optional $n$-gram language model
lowers the pooled WER of H22ctl by \LmEngineGain{} points in the engine (beam~4, from
\LmEngineNoLm\% to \LmEngineWithLm\% on the panel, with weights chosen on DEV) and by
\LmHeldOutGain{} points held out on full DEV in PyTorch. Its \LmPackMb\,MB pack is held in memory,
and no row in our tables uses it. Its training text contains \LmOverlapCount{} of the
\LmOverlapOf{} longer DEV transcripts verbatim; without them the relative gain is unchanged.

\para{Always on, as far as shown.} The always-on tests ran on \AoCheckpoint, not on our
Fig.~\ref{fig:money} checkpoint. When this draft was built the endless run had covered
\AoEndlessHours{} of its \AoStreamHours\,h; reset-with-replay is shown on the stream's first hour
only, and the full run with it is under way. The non-speech audio is digital silence and
synthetic noise, not music, television or babble. The capacity test fed every stream the same
short excerpt of the panel, and its ceilings come from CPU cost, not from a run at that load.

\para{Exploratory measurements.} Every number comes from outside a qualifying window.
\ColdHostSentence{} Each cost is the median of three runs. On the shared host the three runs of
one system spanned a factor of two as the load changed, and on the quiet host other benchmark arms
slowed a recognizer by up to three quarters; this is why rows are now admitted only from an
otherwise idle machine. Absolute speeds on that machine are limited by its memory bus (four of
eight channels populated), so they understate what the same core does with all channels filled,
for every system that streams its weights. The density ladder used 20\,s rungs and a single
repeat, not the frozen three 120\,s repeats with 300\,s confirmations. The qualified run may move
every ratio in either direction.

\para{What the comparison does not cover.} Each recognizer runs in its own released
configuration and native streaming interface; we did not tune the other systems. The cold cost
includes one model load per \ColdAudioMinutes{} minutes of audio, so it measures neither start-up
time nor the cost of short sessions. \ColdLagSentence{} \ColdWattsSentence{} \ColdEightSentence{}
\ColdMachinesSentence{} Two engines of \cref{tab:reported} have no measured row: one needs a
vendor key and restricts benchmarking in its terms of use, and the runtime of the other is
licensed separately.

\para{Domain and data.} All audio is LibriSpeech DEV: read English, in-domain for \ultra and for
the Zipformer baseline, which are trained on LibriSpeech alone. The other systems are trained on
other data whose overlap with LibriSpeech we do not know. A system's rank here says little about
its rank on conversational or noisy speech; the published averages in \cref{tab:reported} cover
more corpora.

\para{Test sets and scope.} All results are DEV; the LibriSpeech test sets and the sealed
VoxPopuli and AMI sets are unread for this paper (earlier test reads are disclosed in
\cref{sec:protocol}). Paced density is measured only for \ultra and Zipformer, in the warm
harness; the other systems have no path in that harness, so they have no density row and no
implied ceiling.
