\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

\para{Release gate.} The \ultra{} release is shipped by owner decision after missing its registered
full-DEV final edit gate (\CandFinalFullEdits{} edits vs.\ \CandGateFinalFull).

\para{Utterance-segmented final mode and memory.} \ultra{} live streams continuously without an endpoint
detector. \ultra{} final needs utterance endpoints (capped at 40\,s), adds a post-endpoint commit delay
(median \RelCommitDelayPfifty\,ms, from an earlier checkpoint of identical cost), and raises peak RSS to
\ColdHeadRss\,MB because of the 5-gram LM pack (\LmPackMb\,MB on disk); shrinking both without losing
accuracy remains future work.

\para{Live accuracy and next lever.} The endpoint-free live stream trades accuracy for extreme speed and
low power: its gap to Zipformer INT8 on the unselected DEV C half is \CandLiveLmCDelta\pp
(\cref{sec:versus}). Most live repair positions (\VfDiagInteriorPct\%) are common interior words on hard
audio, and probes of reranking, regularisation, and relabelling existing audio did not move clean live
WER; further gains at this compute budget will need fresh acoustic diversity and stronger
non-Zipformer supervision.

\para{Training data and LM overlap.} \ultra{} is trained on LibriSpeech 960\,h plus Libri-Light
pseudo-labelled by R0 (the Zipformer comparator). Release comparisons use read English LibriSpeech DEV
audio, which is in-domain for both \ultra{} and Zipformer; rankings on conversational, accented, or
noisy speech may differ. The OpenSLR-11 training text of \texttt{M5.ulm} contains \LmOverlapCount{} of
the \LmOverlapOf{} longer DEV transcripts verbatim; removing them leaves the relative LM WER gain
unchanged.

\para{Host coverage, energy scope, and DEV-only claims.} \ColdHostSentence{} \ColdSubsetSentence{}
Energy is active core (CPU-domain) joules per audio-second from RAPL registers, never board or wall
power, measured unpaced; every plotted system is idle-subtracted in the R7 campaign on a
\RsevenClips-clip subset while CI jobs ran on other cores, and the paired ratio is from the R6 blocks. All accuracy numbers are LibriSpeech DEV only;
as disclosed in \cref{sec:protocol}, test-clean and test-other were read several times before our
benchmark freeze.
