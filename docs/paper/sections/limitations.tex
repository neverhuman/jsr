\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

\para{Exploratory measurements.} Every number in this draft comes from shared hosts outside a
qualifying window. A same-day A/A replay on such a host varied by up to \AASpread\%; the density
ladder used 20\,s rungs and a single repeat rather than the frozen three 120\,s repeats with
300\,s confirmations; and the CPU governor was not controlled. The qualified run may move every ratio
in either direction.

\para{Threats, and what the qualifying window fixes.} The rehearsal's measurement shortcuts each
have a scheduled fix: 120\,s rungs with three repeats and three 300\,s confirmations instead of one
20\,s repeat; a full-machine ladder on 15 CCX; panel audio instead of \LadderUtts{} cycled
utterances; a sweep of Zipformer's best serving configuration (threads, chunk, batching) rather
than its defaults; and a ladder for the beam-4 operating point, which is more accurate and more
expensive than the greedy rows here.

\para{Accuracy.} \ultra is less accurate than the systems it outruns (Zipformer \WerRzeroDevClean\%
and Nemotron \WerNemoDevClean\% on dev-clean; our best dense configuration \WerDtwoBestClean\%), so
the density comparison is between different operating points and is not a better accuracy--cost
trade-off at equal accuracy. Training was unfinished, LibriSpeech-only, and short of the
Zipformer recipe's epochs.

\para{Test sets and scope.} All results are DEV; the LibriSpeech test sets and the sealed
VoxPopuli and AMI sets are unread for this paper (earlier test reads are disclosed in
\cref{sec:protocol}). Density is measured on one CPU family (Zen~2, AVX2, no VNNI or AVX-512);
Nemotron has no path in the density harness and so no density row and no implied ceiling.
