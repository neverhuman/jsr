\begin{abstract}
An always-on speech recogniser pays for every second of audio, speech or not, so its CPU cost, memory
and energy per stream matter as much as its word error rate (WER). \ultra{} (\ReleaseVer{} weights,
published in the v3.2.0 runtime release) pairs a compact streaming Conformer transducer with a
pure-Rust engine built on per-row INT8 quantisation, exact int32 accumulation and a portable log-mel
front end; a single 60\,MB INT8 artifact serves both always-on live streaming and an utterance-end
commit pass. \emph{Speed and energy:} measured on one pinned quiet Zen~2 core across the 730-utterance
LibriSpeech DEV selection panel against all \ColdCompleteCompCount{} complete-panel public open-weights
CPU comparators, live streaming with a compact language model runs at \VsWideSpeed$\times$ real time
and \VsWideWatts\,J/audio-s (\VsWideWer\% WER, 320\,ms chunk) or \ColdLiveSpeed$\times$ and
\ColdLiveWatts\,J/audio-s (\ColdLiveWer\% WER, 160\,ms chunk, \ColdLiveRss\,MB RAM)---\VsWideSpeedX$\times$
and \VsSpeedXOne$\times$ the paired throughput of streaming Zipformer INT8 at \VsWideEnergyPct\% and
\VsEnergyPctOne\% of its active core energy; greedy mode reaches \VsGreedySpeed$\times$ in
\ColdRcTwoLiveGreedyRss\,MB RAM. \emph{Accuracy:} on the unselected half of DEV (C), live +LM trails
Zipformer INT8 by \CandLiveLmEightCDelta\pp (320\,ms) and \CandLiveLmCDelta\pp (160\,ms). At utterance
ends, the commit pass on the same weights reaches \ColdHeadWer\% panel WER (\CandFinalFullWer\% full
DEV), \VsVerdictTwo{} Zipformer's \ColdZipWer\% (paired bounds \VsLoTwo{} to \VsHiTwo\pp), at
\VsSpeedXTwo$\times$ its single-core speed, while opt-in v3.2.0 \texttt{-{}-rms-norm} recovers
\AudioZOneThirtyDelta\pp on C-half quiet speech ($-$30\,dB) at at most \AudioZOneCpuStreamUpper\% CPU.
\ultra{} \ReleaseVer{} is released by owner decision after missing its registered full-DEV final edit
gate (\CandFinalFullEdits{} vs.\ \CandGateFinalFull). \CandIsaRuns{} witness decodes across x86 and ARM
CPUs reproduce its Zen~2 transcripts exactly. All results are exploratory and use development data only.
\end{abstract}
