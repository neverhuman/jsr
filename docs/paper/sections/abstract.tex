\begin{abstract}
An always-on speech recogniser pays for every second of audio, speech or not, so its CPU cost, memory
and energy per stream matter as much as its word error rate (WER). \ultra{} (\ReleaseVer{} weights,
published in the v3.2.0 runtime release) pairs a compact streaming Conformer transducer with a
pure-Rust engine built on per-row INT8 quantisation, exact int32 accumulation and a portable log-mel
front end; a single 60\,MB INT8 artifact serves both always-on live streaming and an utterance-end
commit pass. \emph{Speed and energy:} measured on one pinned quiet Zen~2 core across the 730-utterance
LibriSpeech DEV selection panel against all \ColdCompleteCompCount{} complete-panel public open-weights
CPU comparators, live streaming with a compact language model at 160\,ms runs at \ColdLiveSpeed$\times$
real time and \ColdLiveWatts\,J/audio-s (\ColdLiveWer\% WER, \ColdLiveRss\,MB RAM)---\VsSpeedXOne$\times$
the paired throughput of streaming Zipformer INT8 at \VsEnergyPctOne\% of its active core energy. The
same weights at a 320\,ms chunk run at \VsWideSpeed$\times$ and \VsWideWatts\,J/audio-s, and greedy
decoding reaches \VsGreedySpeed$\times$ in \ColdRcTwoLiveGreedyRss\,MB RAM (\cref{tab:measured}).
\emph{Accuracy:} on the unselected half of DEV (C), the 160\,ms live +LM stream trails Zipformer INT8
by \CandLiveLmCDelta\pp (320\,ms: \CandLiveLmEightCDelta\pp). At utterance ends, the commit pass on the
same weights reaches \ColdHeadWer\% panel WER (\CandFinalFullWer\% full DEV), \VsVerdictTwo{}
Zipformer's \ColdZipWer\% (paired bounds \VsLoTwo{} to \VsHiTwo\pp), at \VsSpeedXTwo$\times$ its
single-core speed. \ultra{} \ReleaseVer{} is released by owner decision after missing its registered full-DEV final edit
gate (\CandFinalFullEdits{} vs.\ \CandGateFinalFull). \CandIsaRuns{} witness decodes across x86 and ARM
CPUs reproduce its Zen~2 transcripts exactly. All results are exploratory and use development data only.
\end{abstract}
