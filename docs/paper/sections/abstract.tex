final text. On one pinned quiet Zen~2 core, \ultra{} live (160\,ms, +LM) reaches
\ColdLiveSpeed$\times$ real time, \ColdLiveWer\% word error rate (WER), and

its idle-subtracted core energy in paired full-panel blocks.
