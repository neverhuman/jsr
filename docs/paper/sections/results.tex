\subsection{Head-to-Head with Zipformer INT8 and Full-DEV Accuracy}\label{sec:versus}

Zipformer INT8 is the strongest public streaming comparator near our cost point and the teacher of our
pseudo-labels, so we compare against it directly on the same utterances and core (\cref{tab:versus}),
timed in the same three interleaved blocks on quiet H1 (R10 for v4.2.1 live, R9 for the v4.2.0 final pass); speed, energy and memory ratios are medians of
within-block ratios, and WER differences are paired with speaker-cluster bootstrap. Because the
730-utterance panel overlaps the selection half of DEV, we also report the unselected confirmation half
(C, \LookCUtts{} utterances), decoded once for each frozen release: there, \ultra{} live is \LivCZipDelta\pp from
Zipformer (bounds \LivCZipLo{} to \LivCZipHi), lower WER; before the v4.2.1 endpoint rerank it was \LookLiveCZipDelta\pp
(bounds \LookLiveCZipLo{} to \LookLiveCZipHi), not a significant difference. \ultra{} live uses \VsCpuPctOne\% of Zipformer's CPU time
(\VsSpeedXOne$\times$ throughput), \VsEnergyPctOne\% of its active core energy (paired R10 blocks), and \VsRssPctOne\% of its
memory with half the chunk (\VsChunkOne\,ms vs.\ \VsZipChunk\,ms); its panel WER is \VsVerdictOne{}
Zipformer's (\VsDeltaOne\pp, bounds \VsLoOne{} to \VsHiOne\pp). \ultra{} final is \VsVerdictTwo{}
Zipformer's (\VsDeltaTwo\pp, bounds \VsLoTwo{} to \VsHiTwo\pp; lower on C at \FinCZipDelta\pp, bounds \FinCZipLo{} to \FinCZipHi) at
\VsSpeedXTwo$\times$ its single-core speed and \VsEnergyPctTwo\% of its active core energy.

\para{What v4.2.1 changes.} Only live mode changed. When a live input ends (each utterance file here; the
continuous \texttt{isosyll stream} mode is unchanged) and the beam's top-1 and top-2 scores differ by less than \LivSkipMargin
(about \LivRoutedPct\% of utterance ends in research), \ultra{} rescores the live beam with the frozen
content-word t2 reranker of the final pass (left-to-right transformer, M5 LM and content-word features; weights refit
on live beams) and commits its choice; otherwise it keeps the beam's best. Against v4.1.0/v4.2.0 live on the same
utterances, v4.2.1 live is \LivPanelPrevDelta\pp on the panel (bounds \LivPanelPrevLo{} to \LivPanelPrevHi), \LivCPrevDelta\pp
on the unselected C half (bounds \LivCPrevLo{} to \LivCPrevHi; dev-clean \LivCClean\%), and on the selection half it
reproduces the research list (\LivSResDelta\pp vs.\ \LivResSWer\%, bounds \LivSResLo{} to \LivSResHi). In the paired R10
blocks it changes CPU time per audio-second by \LivCostCpuPct\%, core energy by \LivCostEnergyPct\% and peak RSS by
+\LivCostRssMb\,MB (live mode now also loads the final pass's LM and rescorer) against the published v4.2.0 live
binary, and adds \LivDelayPFifty\,ms median and \LivDelayPNinetyFive\,ms p95 of single-core compute at each utterance end
(paired per-file wall time, decode unpaced).

\para{What v4.2.0 changed.} Only the final pass changed. Against the v4.1.0 final pass on the same utterances,
v4.2.0 final is \FinPanelPrevDelta\pp on the panel (bounds \FinPanelPrevLo{} to \FinPanelPrevHi), \FinCPrevDelta\pp on
the unselected C half (bounds \FinCPrevLo{} to \FinCPrevHi; dev-clean \FinPrevCClean\% $\to$ \FinCClean\%), and the gated beam-48
re-search alone is \FinSBaseDelta\pp on the selection half against the same pass without it (bounds \FinSBaseLo{} to
\FinSBaseHi). In the paired R9 blocks the whole pass changes CPU time per audio-second by \FinCostCpuPct\% and core
energy by \FinCostEnergyPct\% against the released v4.1.0 final binary; the gate alone costs \FinGateCpuPct\% CPU and
\FinGateEnergyPct\% energy against the same build with the gate off, at the +\FinGateBudgetPct\% budget set for it (locked by owner choice when
research timing put it just over).

On full LibriSpeech DEV (\RelFullUtts{} utterances, which contains the selection half and is therefore
descriptive), \ultra{} live reaches \LivFullClean\% dev-clean, \LivFullOther\% dev-other
and \LivFullWer\% pooled WER (v4.1.0 live: \LookLiveFullWer\%); \ultra{} final reaches \FinFullClean\% dev-clean, \FinFullOther\% dev-other and
\FinFullWer\% pooled (v4.1.0 final: \LookFinalFullWer\%).
