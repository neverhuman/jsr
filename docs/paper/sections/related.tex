\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\para{Streaming transducers and chunked attention.} The RNN transducer~\cite{graves2012rnnt}
became the default streaming recogniser after it ran in real time on a phone~\cite{he2019streaming}. Self-attention encoders were made
streamable by restricting each frame's context. Transformer Transducer limits the left and right
context~\cite{zhang2020transformertransducer}. Chunk masks with cached history decode in fixed
blocks~\cite{chen2021developing}, and WeNet trains one model over dynamic chunk
sizes~\cite{yao2021wenet}. Emformer carries an augmented memory across
segments~\cite{shi2021emformer}, and cache-aware FastConformer removes the gap between how a model
is trained and how it is streamed~\cite{noroozi2024stateful}. Zipformer~\cite{yao2024zipformer} improves on the Conformer~\cite{gulati2020conformer} by running
middle stacks at lower frame rates. Pruned RNN-T~\cite{kuang2022pruned}, a stateless
predictor~\cite{ghodsi2020stateless} and consistency-regularised CTC~\cite{yao2025crctc} cut
training cost. \ultra is a chunked Conformer transducer built from these parts. Our multirate
encoder applies Zipformer's insight that not every block needs the full frame rate inside
fixed 160\,ms streaming chunks. We claim the measurement of multirate as a lever on CPU
stream density, not the idea of multirate itself.

\para{The systems we measure.} Whisper~\cite{radford2023whisper} spans five sizes. Its encoder
always processes a padded 30\,s window, so a short utterance costs as much as a long one;
whisper.cpp~\cite{whispercpp} runs it on CPUs, and its streaming mode re-runs the model on
successive windows. Moonshine~\cite{moonshine} scales encoder compute with input length, and
Moonshine v2~\cite{kudlur2026moonshinev2} adds a sliding-window streaming encoder in three sizes.
NVIDIA's FastConformer~\cite{rekesh2023fastconformer} uses 8\x subsampling. It underlies
Parakeet-TDT~\cite{xu2023tdt,parakeettdt,nvidia2025parakeettdtv3}, a whole-utterance model, and the
cache-aware Nemotron streaming models~\cite{nemotron,nvidia2026nemotron35}, which
NeMo-Speech.cpp~\cite{nemospeechcpp} runs on CPUs. Parakeet Redux~\cite{moondream2026parakeetredux}
is a version of Parakeet TDT v3 with ternary encoder weights and its own runtime, whose x86 fast
path needs AVX-512 VNNI. Zipformer~\cite{yao2024zipformer} streaming models are deployed through
sherpa-onnx~\cite{sherpaonnx,icefall}, and Kaldi~\cite{povey2011kaldi} models through
Vosk~\cite{vosk}. Picovoice Cheetah is a commercial streaming engine whose terms of use set conditions on publishing benchmarks of it;
it appears here only with its vendor's own numbers~\cite{picovoice2026benchmark}. Parakeet Redux's
runtime is licensed separately, so it too appears only with its vendor's numbers. Self-supervised encoders
such as wav2vec~2.0~\cite{baevski2020wav2vec} are larger still. These works report WER together
with parameters, FLOPs or a speed figure on the authors' own machine. \Cref{tab:reported} collects
what they and their benchmarks publish, and \cref{tab:measured} what we measure for the same
systems on one machine.

\para{CPU inference and quantisation.} Integer-arithmetic-only inference~\cite{jacob2018quantization}
and its extensions to Transformers~\cite{kim2021ibert} established INT8 with int32 accumulation.
Surveys cover the practical choices of granularity and calibration~\cite{wu2020integer,nagel2021whitepaper},
and LLM.int8() scales activations per row~\cite{dettmers2022llmint8}. Ternary and 1.58-bit
weights~\cite{wang2023bitnet,ma2024bitnet} promise large memory savings. Our negative result
(\cref{sec:negative}) shows that the memory saving does not by itself buy batch throughput on a
compute-bound CPU. Deployed CPU ASR mostly runs through general runtimes: ONNX
Runtime~\cite{onnxruntime} behind sherpa-onnx~\cite{sherpaonnx}, the next-gen Kaldi deployment of
icefall Zipformers~\cite{icefall}, or ggml-based runtimes such as NeMo-Speech.cpp~\cite{nemospeechcpp}.
He et al.\ traced nondeterministic LLM serving to batch-variant kernels~\cite{he2025nondeterminism}.
In \ultra, every activation row is quantised from its own range and accumulation is exact. As a
result, a stream's tokens do not depend on its batch partners, and the engine produces
bit-identical tokens on AVX2 and NEON. We are not aware of an ASR engine that documents
either property.

\para{Benchmarking practice.} MLPerf Inference~\cite{reddi2020mlperf} judges servers by the throughput
they hold under a tail-latency bound, not by a single query's speed. Stream density is the
streaming-ASR form of that rule. ESB~\cite{gandhi2022esb} standardises WER across domains. Speed is
not standardised at all. The comparisons that practitioners consult are a vendor's
benchmark~\cite{picovoice2026benchmark}, a runtime's own benchmark page~\cite{nvidia2026nemospeechbench},
a model family's documentation~\cite{moonshine2026docs} and a model
card~\cite{moondream2026parakeetredux}; each uses a different host, thread count, audio set and text
normaliser (\cref{tab:reported}), and two of them differ by more than a factor of two on the same model
(\cref{sec:compare}). We retracted one of our own density ratios for exactly this kind of mismatch
(\cref{sec:protocol}). We therefore measure every system we compare against under one protocol, on
one host, and report WER beside each cost. Timing contrasts use Holm
correction~\cite{holm1979simple}, and accuracy gates use bootstrap
intervals~\cite{bisani2004bootstrap,efron1994bootstrap}.
