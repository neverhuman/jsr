# ULTRA v3.0.0 release record

**Status: DRAFT.** paper-lane wrote this on 2026-11-23 at about 14:00Z, before the release candidate existed. Cells marked *pending* are filled in from posted receipts once eval-lane and bench-open measure the candidate (about 16:00Z). The tag `v3.0.0` names this release of model, decoder and scorer. The crate versions stay at the workspace's `0.1.0`.

**Labels.** Every number here is **exploratory**. All of them come from **LibriSpeech DEV**: full dev-clean plus dev-other, 5,567 utterances, 106,054 words. The test sets are sealed and were never read. Nothing here comes from a qualifying window under [`FREEZE.md`](../FREEZE.md). Training used Libri-Light audio labelled by R0, so the model is **semi-supervised (R0 labels)**. Rows that use the n-gram LM are marked **+LM**.

## Summary
- **One artifact.** v3.0.0 is one artifact: weights, decoder and scorer, measured together under the H43 contract. Its WER and its speed come from the same weights on the same binary.
- **Model: S2, provisional until the release candidate is picked.**
  - **Recipe:** B0 (A1 step 49,000, EMA) plus delay penalty 0.005, trained further on LibriSpeech 960 h and on Libri-Light small (378.65 h) and medium (3,395.81 h). The Libri-Light audio is pseudo-labelled by R0.
  - **The candidate is RC1's EMA weights.** RC1 is S2's release cooldown. On the selection half, raw − EMA was +0.027 pp, and the rule needed raw to win by at least 0.05 pp (eval-lane 15:02:09Z).
  - **Gate (b) passed** at 13:24:51Z. S2b's branch-point EMA beat B0 by −0.72 / −0.78 / −0.71 pp at chunks 4 / 8 / 16.
  - **If RC1 fails its A6 look,** v3.0.0 ships B0 with the same decoder (row A below), and this record will say so.
- **Decoder: a greedy stream plus an endpoint commit pass (+LM).**
  - **Stream:** greedy decoding at chunk 4 (160 ms) produces the live, provisional text.
  - **Commit pass:** when the caller declares an endpoint, the closed segment is decoded again at chunk 16 (640 ms). This pass uses beam 16, the M5 token 5-gram and LODR (LM 0.80 / LODR 0.60), a 320 ms tail pad and a 40 s cap per segment.
  - **How it ships:** as the `CommitPassSession` library API in `ultra-engine`, and as the `ultra-engine-commit` eval CLI (built at main bb1071a), which reads pre-segmented audio.
  - **v3.0.0 has no live endpointer for the commit pass.** Integrating one is the first v3.1 item.
- **Measured on B0 with the release decoder** (the fallback):
  - **Final text, utterance-segmented** (the LibriSpeech segment boundaries serve as endpoints): 6.087% WER on full dev. That is −2.186 pp [−2.435, −1.943] against B0's stream, and release gate E1 passes.
  - **Live streaming WER** (the greedy chunk-4 provisional text, which needs no endpoint): 8.272%. This is B0's INT8 stream, text for text.
  - **Speed:** 14.3× real time on one quiet core (0.0702 CPU-s per audio-s, median of 3 rounds).
  - **Memory:** 686 MiB peak RSS, most of it the LM.
  - **Final-text delay:** the final text arrives p50 210 ms / p95 589 ms after the endpoint is declared.
- **Measured on the candidate with the release decoder:** *pending*.
  - **For comparison, the S2 trajectory on the same binary** (final text, utterance-segmented, full dev): B0 6.087% → S2-BP1 5.898% → S2b-BP1 5.492%.
  - These are uncooled branch points, not release rows. S2b-BP1's live stream scores 7.579%.
- **Opt-in features, off by default:** the Sentinel-0 sleep gate, warm AGC and chunk-coalesced wakes. Warm AGC failed its stream criterion (C3) at 14:05Z, so it stays opt-in.

## Read this first: the final text is utterance-segmented
- **The headline WER is final text on pre-segmented audio.**
  - The commit pass decodes a segment again only when its caller declares an endpoint (`CommitPassSession::commit_endpoint`).
  - Its one binary, `ultra-engine-commit`, reads one file per LibriSpeech utterance and commits at the end of each file.
  - So every final-text WER in this record uses the LibriSpeech segment boundaries as endpoints.
- **No v3.0.0 binary finds endpoints on live audio for the commit pass.** A deployment has to supply its own endpoints, and its final-text WER can differ from these numbers. An endpointer that cuts mid-word or merges utterances was not measured. (`ultra stream --policy commit` is a different thing: the word-ledger output policy.)
- **The live streaming WER** is the greedy chunk-4 text that the user sees as audio arrives. It needs no endpoint, and every table below reports it beside the final text.
  - On B0 it is 8.272%, decoding each utterance from a fresh stream.
  - On the 27.56 h continuous DEV stream (always-on C1, reset-with-replay, no oracle segmentation) it is 8.377%.
- **Integrating a live endpointer is the first v3.1 item.**

**What the LM costs on B0** (14m, same binary and panel, medians of 3 rounds):
- **Without the LM:** a greedy chunk-16 final pass costs 0.0648 CPU-s per audio-s (15.5×), uses 251 MiB, draws 0.67 / 1.67 W of core / package per stream and delivers the final text p50 180 / p95 499 ms after the endpoint.
- **With beam 16 + LM:** +0.0054 CPU-s per audio-s (+8%), +435 MiB, and the final text arrives +30 ms later at p50 and +90 ms later at p95.
- **WER without the LM:** the greedy final pass's full-dev final-text WER (utterance-segmented) at pad 320 is 7.677% (8,142 edits). That was measured on #76's binary, which had a 30 s cap, so it is not the timed binary.

**How these numbers are measured:**
- **WER:** the canonical scorer `ultra-score` (sha256 d784f8ca…, normaliser `ultra-en-wer-norm/v1`, rule table 988c59ac…). Pooled WER is the edits summed over both dev sets divided by 106,054 words.
  - Both texts are scored per utterance on full dev.
  - The live stream text is the provisional text when each utterance ends. The final text is the commit pass's output at the utterance's end.
- **Paired deltas:** paired speaker-cluster bootstrap, 10,000 replicates, seed 20261122. Brackets are one-sided 95% bounds unless stated otherwise.
- **Speed:** one process with one GEMM thread on one quiet Zen 2 core of x0, with the owner's timers paused. The input is the 730-utterance DEV panel (5,259.6 s), unpaced, in 20 ms packets, and every utterance ends in an endpoint commit.
  - "Stream" is the chunk-4 provisional decode.
  - "Final pass" is the chunk-16 commit pass.
  - × real time is 1 / (CPU-s per audio-s).
- **Final-text delay:** the wall time of the final pass after the endpoint is declared.
  - The endpointer's own silence wait is not included.
  - The 320 ms tail pad is synthetic.
  - The delay grows with segment length: the median of the three runs' maxima is 1,127 ms, on a 31.8 s utterance.
- **Peak RSS:** `/usr/bin/time` maximum RSS of that process. It includes the 334 MB M5 LM.
- **Watts:** net joules per audio-second, taken from energy's 1 Hz log of the core energy register (core) and of the package counter (package). The floor is 60 s of idle before and after each run.
  - This is the incremental cost of one stream on a CPU domain. **It is not wall, supply or device power.**
  - The package floor drifts by ±1-2 W within a hold.
- **First-token latency:** both release rows decode the provisional stream greedily at chunk 4. On row A that stream is B0's own, text for text (5,567 / 5,567).
  - **B0's stream on full dev** (design clock, eval-lane H43): first token 312 ms after the end of the first word (mean; p95 480 ms). First correct word 354 ms after its end and 616 ms after its onset (means). 32.6% of words are correct and stable within 300 ms.
  - **Row B's stream** is the candidate's own, and its latency is part of the A6 look (*pending*).

## What changed since v2
"v2" means the state before v3: the ULTRA-S A1 weights in the native INT8 engine, greedy 160 ms streaming, and the n-gram LM optional and off in every headline row. B0 = A1 step 49,000 EMA is v2's final checkpoint.

1. **Endpoint commit pass with the LM in the final pass only** (#76, #106, #107). Measured on the same B0 weights:
   - **WER:** 8.272% (live stream) → 6.087% (final text, utterance-segmented) with the full release decoder. The commit pass with a greedy final pass gives 7.677% on its own (measured on #76's binary, 30 s cap).
   - **How it ships:** as the `CommitPassSession` API and the `ultra-engine-commit` eval CLI. There is no live endpointer yet (v3.1).
   - **Text stability:** the stream stays greedy, so the 160 ms partials and the stream's latency are unchanged.
     - Partial text is almost never revised mid-segment: B0's greedy stream retracts 0.09 words per 100, against 4.48 for streaming L4.
     - The final text replaces the provisional text at the endpoint, and 45% of segments change there.
   - **Why not the LM in the stream:** in streaming L4 the beam keeps rewriting partial text, and E2 fails.
   - **Cost:** about 2.1× the bare stream (0.0702 against 0.0339 CPU-s per audio-s in the same process).
   - **Commit cap:** #107 raised the cap per segment from 30 s to 40 s. Segments longer than the cap keep their chunk-4 greedy text.
2. **New weights from new data** (H52, H53). S2 adds R0-labelled Libri-Light to LibriSpeech 960 h.
   - **More 960 h alone made B0 worse.** A1C (B0 plus 8,000 replay steps at dp 0) was worse by +0.08 / +0.14 / +0.19 pp on full dev at chunks 4 / 8 / 16.
   - **S2a** (960 h + small) plateaued at −0.31 pp against B0 at chunk 4.
   - **S2b** (+ medium, the Libri-Light share capped at 0.5 of the audio) reached −0.72 pp after 10k audio-h.
   - **Delay penalty 0.005** is in the recipe for its latency gain. The blind criterion: first token −76.5 ms [−80.6, −72.5] for A1L against A1C at chunk 4 on full dev. Its WER effect at S2 scale was never isolated, because the S2 factorial that would have tested it was cut to v3.1.
3. **Engine correctness fixes** (#72; the H44 wave-0 defects):
   - a configurable predictor context;
   - the BlankCert bypass is disabled under quantised activations until parity is proven;
   - beam resets keep their context start and LM state;
   - only a shared beam prefix is committed, and a committed-prefix revision fails loudly.
4. **Measurement** (#102, H43): one canonical scorer and alignment ledger, two latency clocks (end and onset of the first word), correct-by-deadline metrics, and quiet-host holds on x0 with the owner's timers paused.
5. **Opt-in features** (see the next section): the Sentinel-0 sleep gate (#77), warm AGC (#104, #108) and chunk-coalesced wakes (#105).
6. **Merged but not used by v3.0.0's defaults:**
   - the AVX-512 VNNI kernels (#89, opt-in through `--isa` in the engine's eval binaries);
   - the speculative onset peek (#88);
   - the compact FFNN LM runtime and trainer (#79, #100);
   - the endless-stream LM flags (#97);
   - the fleet tools: dashboard v3 (#83), the DiLoCo hub and launchers (#73, #74, #103), and the guards and auditors (#82, #86, #92).

## Limits (read these before quoting a number)
- **DEV only.** The test sets are sealed. Everything is exploratory, and nothing comes from a FREEZE qualifying window.
- **Watts are CPU domains, not devices.** They are core-register and package-counter energy on one quiet Zen 2 host (x0), net of idle, per stream. Wall, DRAM and supply power are not included. Whole-device watts wait for the owner's metered target device. The registered gross-energy endpoint (E3, 5% duty) is not measured yet.
- **Level sensitivity (B0 family).** On the 730-utterance gain panel (B0 INT8, 160 ms, isolated utterances), WER rises +1.14 pp [+0.87, +1.41] at −20 dB and +3.05 pp [+2.58, +3.54] at −30 dB. First-word error goes from 8.49% at 0 dB to 11.51% at −20 dB and 16.44% at −30 dB.
  - S2 was trained without gain augmentation (dropped at 10:20Z), so there is no reason to expect S2 to be less level-sensitive. It was not measured on the candidate.
- **Warm AGC on mixed streams.** On the 27.56 h stream it costs +0.447 pp, and 121 of 164 pieces got worse.
  - **Likely cause** (eval-lane, post hoc): the noise gaps sit above the −65 dBFS speech floor, so the AGC's level estimate tracks the noise and shifts the gain applied to the next speech.
- **Warm AGC from a cold start.**
  - The AGC helps after a few seconds of speech at a new level, not on the first word after power-on.
  - A cold AGC per clip recovers 12-13% of the WER loss and 0% of the first-word loss.
  - A cold start needs about 45 s of speech to reach a −30 dB speaker.
  - The seed has no cap yet: a speech-free pre-roll above −65 dBFS can start the gain at up to +40 dB.
- **Streaming L4 fails E2.** The protected paced first-correct-word p95 is +40.1 ms [−159.8, +320.4] against B0, and the registered point rule allows +20 ms.
  - The release decoder avoids this by keeping the stream greedy.
  - At n ≈ 73 this p95 is fragile. On the utterances both arms cover, L4 is 60 ms earlier.
- **The sleep gate loses first words in loud noise** (Q4b, 13,400 items, 29 cells).
  - The loss sits in decoding, not in waking: each segment restarts with 500 ms of pre-roll.
  - It is worst for voiced fricatives (−2.00 pp) and short gaps (0.4 s: −1.63 pp).
  - **False words:** in 30 h of non-speech, 0 words. On the stream's gaps, one added word put the upper bound at 0.1008 per hour. Q5b (≥ 50 h of fresh data) was still running on the Mac when its owner lane went unstaffed at 10:43Z. No result has been posted.
- **Long sessions: no growth found.** The release decoder's CPU and memory stay flat over long sessions (receipts of 35-88 minutes).
  - **bench-open, 14m:** each run is one 87.7-minute session of 730 utterances, with a full reset after each. Every 10-minute window cost 0.0696-0.0701 CPU-s per audio-s.
  - **runtime-lane:** one 35-minute continuous `CommitPassSession` with no full reset, over 270 endpoints. RSS held at 919 MB, and the steady state was 0.074-0.075 CPU-s per audio-s on a shared H0 core.
  - **The only thing that grows** is the session's committed text, about 27 KB per 35 minutes. Capping it is a minor v3.1 item.
- **The final text is utterance-segmented** (see "Read this first"). v3.0.0 has no live endpointer, and every final-text WER uses the LibriSpeech segment boundaries as endpoints.
- **Final-text delay** excludes the endpointer's silence wait. The tail pad is synthetic. Segments over 40 s keep their greedy chunk-4 text.
- **Installed size.** B0's INT8 bundle directory holds 283.8 MB. That includes the FP32 weights (227.0 MB), because the INT8 engine still opens them. The M5 LM adds 334.4 MB.
- **Selection.** The LM weights (0.80 / 0.60) and beam 16 were chosen on the selection half of dev, so full dev counts as one look. The decoder settings were fixed on B0 and are applied to the candidate unchanged.
- **Training deviations, disclosed:**
  - S2b ran at window_groups 96 (Amendment 6.1). The pooled Libri-Light share stayed at 0.50, but the per-batch p95 was 0.83 against the 0.65 target.
  - The H52 small-data pilot was INCONCLUSIVE (−0.096 pp, under the 0.17 bar). S2 ran provisionally, with gate (b) as the deciding test.

## Not in v3.0.0 (deferred to v3.1)
- **A live endpointer for the commit pass. This is the first v3.1 item.** Drive `CommitPassSession` from a real endpointer on live audio, then measure final-text WER and delay with realistic segmentation.
- Gain-augmented training. It can't enter S2 without a matched control.
- The S2 factorial, including dp 0.005's blind WER confirmation.
- PQ / int4 kernels. Staged PQ was NO-GO for the registered target (H51).
- The compact LM: the ≤ 8 MiB ladder and FFNN-6.
- The H48 low-latency models (A1L, 80 ms mode, anchored loss).
- The DiLoCo islands. The D0 / D1 proof is shelved: the transport defect, a blocking `POST /delta` with a 60 s client timeout during the hub's outer commit, is analysed in `/mnt/xbabe3-external/jsr/h50-fleet/D0D1/TRANSPORT_POSTMORTEM.md`.
- Minor clean-ups from code review, with no measured cost:
  - cap or rotate the session's committed text, which grows about 27 KB per 35 minutes (`accept_i16` clones it on every packet);
  - bounded incremental detokenisation.
- Warm AGC across non-speech gaps: a level estimate that ignores noise between utterances, then a fresh C1-C4. Also the seed cap (±20 dB suggested).
- For the sleep gate: a longer pre-roll or warm context when the noise floor is high, then a fresh Q4 / Q5.
- For chunk-coalesced wakes: run the 30 s cap check at the packet that crosses 480,000 samples, not at the next chunk wake.
