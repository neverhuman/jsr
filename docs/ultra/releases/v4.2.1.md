# Isosyll v4.2.1 (2026-11-27): lower live WER with an utterance-end rerank

**Status:** Release candidate, 2026-11-27 (MST). The owner chose this setting ("ship H318 as v4.2.1 with the timed 59% setting") from research timing; its measured R10 cost (below) is well above that estimate, and publication is held for the owner's confirmation. Exploratory DEV-only results. The final pass is unchanged from v4.2.0. v4.2.0 stays published as the rollback target.

## What changed
- **Live endpoint rerank (research H318).** When a live input ends (`isosyll transcribe --mode live`; each file is one
  input), isosyll looks at the live beam. If its top two hypothesis scores differ by less than **3.75** (about 59% of
  DEV utterance ends in research), it rescores the beam with the frozen content-word reranker of the v4.2.0 final pass:
  the 12 MB left-to-right transformer LM (`lm/t2.utfr`), the M5 LM (`lm/final.ulm`) and the content-word features
  (`final/content-lexicon.json`). Its weights are refit on live beams (`live-content-fit.json`, sha256 `5a36e403…`,
  research H305/H318 `fitLive_s375.json`). Duplicate texts are merged (log-add) before the choice. Otherwise the beam's
  best text is kept, as in v4.2.0.
- **Unchanged:** the live acoustic model and decoder (v4.1.0's H247 weights, 160 ms chunks, beam 12, compact M5 live LM),
  the v4.2.0 final pass (`isosyll transcribe`, the default), and `isosyll stream` (continuous, with the v4.1.0 commit
  pass; it does not use the rerank).
- **Memory:** live mode now also loads the M5 LM and t2 rescorer it shares with the final pass (+329 MB peak RSS on the
  timed panel run). The model archive adds one 707-byte file; it is otherwise the v4.2.0 archive.
- Commands and platforms are unchanged.

## DEV accuracy (LibriSpeech DEV only; ultra-score, 10,000 speaker-cluster bootstrap replicates, seed 20261122)
The rerank was selected on the DEV selection half (S, 2,782 utterances) in research; the unselected half (C, 2,785
utterances) was decoded once with the release binary.

| Split | v4.2.1 live pooled | dev-clean | dev-other | v4.1.0/v4.2.0 live | v4.2.1 − v4.2.0 live [one-sided 95% bounds] |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| panel (730) | 4.58% | 2.74% | 7.22% | 5.08% | −0.50 [−0.66, −0.34] pp |
| S half | 4.91% | 2.39% | 7.74% | 5.43% | −0.52 [−0.61, −0.43] pp |
| C half (unselected) | 4.67% | 2.66% | 6.70% | 5.22% | −0.55 [−0.66, −0.46] pp |

- **Full DEV (5,567 utterances; descriptive, contains S):** live 4.79% (2.53% clean, 7.20% other), 5,078 edits
  (v4.2.0 live 5.32%).
- **Against Zipformer INT8 (paired):** panel −0.67 [−0.99, −0.38] pp; C half −0.46 [−0.66, −0.28] pp. Live is now lower
  on both (v4.2.0 live on C: +0.09 [−0.12, +0.30] pp, not significant).
- **Registered gate (from v3.1.0):** live panel edits 662 ≤ 722, **pass** (v4.1.0/v4.2.0 live: 734, miss). The final pass
  is v4.2.0's and keeps its passes (panel 613 ≤ 650, full DEV 4,580 ≤ 5,489).
- **Research parity:** on the S half the release binary gives 4.9103% against the research H318 native decode's 4.9161%
  (paired −0.006 [−0.025, +0.013] pp); 2,742 of 2,782 transcripts are identical. All 40 differences come from the
  cross-platform rotary-position tables of the t2 rescorer that v4.2.0 introduced (f64 rounded to f32): the same source
  with only that change reverted (a scratch build, never shipped) reproduced the research decode on 2,782/2,782.

## Speed, energy and delay (R10, quiet host H1, one pinned Zen 2 core)
R10 repeats the R8/R9 energy protocol on the full 730-utterance panel: one pinned core of the quiet Zen 2 host (its SMT
sibling idle), 60 s idle windows and three counterbalanced blocks of four arms: the v4.2.1 live build, the published
v4.2.0 binary in live mode, and the R8 anchors (v4.0.0 live +LM default and Zipformer INT8). Pre-registration,
receipts, per-file outputs and the driver log are in
[`docs/paper/data/raw/release_v421/timing_interleaved/`](../../paper/data/raw/release_v421/timing_interleaved/README.md).

- **Valid:** the block-1 anchors reproduced R6 within the pre-registered 5%: v4.0.0 live +LM 0.3267 J/audio-s (R6 0.328,
  −0.4%) and Zipformer INT8 0.5815 (R6 0.573, +1.5%). The block-1 live anchor was core-unclean once and its one rerun
  was clean; every arm is core-clean in all three blocks (the unclean run is kept in the receipts). The host's four
  idle CI runner VMs stayed up throughout.

| Arm (730 utterances, 5,259.6 audio-s) | × real time (CPU) | Core J/audio-s | Peak RSS | Core-clean blocks |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| v4.2.1 live (endpoint rerank) | 28.5× | 0.366 | 659 MB | 1, 2, 3 |
| v4.2.0 live (published binary) | 30.7× | 0.335 | 330 MB | 1, 2, 3 |
| v4.0.0 live +LM (anchor) | 31.9× | 0.327 | 109 MB | 1, 2, 3 |
| Zipformer INT8 (anchor) | 13.9× | 0.580 | 152 MB | 1, 2, 3 |

- **Speed:** CPU-seconds per audio-second, median of the three blocks. **Energy:** idle-subtracted AMD core-energy
  registers (not package, board or wall power), median of core-clean blocks.
- **Cost of the rerank (median of within-block ratios, against v4.2.0 live):** **+7.5% CPU** (blocks +9.5, +7.5,
  +6.8%), **+9.4% core energy** and **×2.0 peak RSS (+329 MB)**, for −0.50 pp panel WER (−0.55 pp on the unselected
  C half).
- **Added utterance-end delay** (paired per-file wall time of the 2,187 file runs, single core, file mode): median
  **+11.0 ms**, p95 **+65.8 ms**, mean +17.6 ms; per-file wall p95 599 ms against 531 ms. It was not measured in a paced
  microphone stream, and `isosyll stream` does not use the rerank.
- **Against the research estimate:** the setting was chosen from paced ARM research timing (H314, two ABBA pairs) of
  about +3% process CPU and +1.6 to +2.6 ms endpoint p95. R10 measures more than twice that CPU cost and about 25× that
  p95 delay. The added CPU time per audio-second (about 0.0025 CPU-s)
  is within research H318's own bound of ≤ 0.003. These figures are reported as measured; the setting was not retuned.
- **Why memory doubles:** the live process now loads the final pass's M5 LM (`lm/final.ulm`, 334 MB on disk, decoded
  into heap memory at load) and the 12 MB t2 rescorer next to its own live LM (`lm/live.ulm`). The +329 MB matches that file.
- **Against Zipformer INT8 (paired, median of within-block ratios):** v4.2.1 live has 2.04× its throughput at 63% of
  its core energy per audio-second, and 4.3× its peak RSS.

## Install and run
```bash
curl -fsSL https://github.com/neverhuman/jsr/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh
isosyll transcribe meeting.wav             # final text (v4.2.0 final pass)
isosyll transcribe --mode live meeting.wav # 160 ms live text with the v4.2.1 utterance-end rerank
```
Pin this release with `install.sh --version v4.2.1`; roll back with `--version v4.2.0`.
