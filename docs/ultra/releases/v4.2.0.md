# Isosyll v4.2.0 (2026-11-27): lower final WER for 10% more final-pass CPU

**Status:** Released 2026-11-27 (MST) by owner decision. Exploratory DEV-only results. The owner locked the final pass's beam-48 confidence gate when research timing put it just over the +5% CPU budget set for it; on the release timing (R9) it lands at that budget, +5.0% (see [Speed and energy](#speed-and-energy-r9-quiet-host-h1-one-pinned-zen-2-core)). The locked research figure on the DEV selection half was 4.4582% WER; the released binary measures 4.4390% because of a precision fix for cross-platform determinism (below).

## What changed
- **New final pass (the `isosyll transcribe` default, `--mode final`).** It uses its own acoustic model in `final/`:
  `final/weights.int8.safetensors` (60 MB, sha256 `5c5bfad9…`), research run H265, a 0.30/0.55/0.15 weight average of
  three same-architecture research checkpoints (H234, H255 and H256, each at step 2,000), with its own `final/arch.json`
  (`5ff4c76a…`). The pass re-encodes the segment with 1,280 ms chunks (32 frames) and a 320 ms tail pad, then runs a
  beam-16 search with the M5 LM (0.80, LODR 0.60), as before.
- **Confidence-gated beam 48.** When the top two fused hypothesis scores of the beam-16 search differ by less than
  0.6467 (a margin fitted to route the 30% least-confident *training* utterances), the search is re-run at beam 48 on the
  cached encoder output.
- **Content-word reranker.** The final n-best list is rescored by a 12 MB frozen left-to-right transformer LM
  (`lm/t2.utfr`, `2bce52ea…`) through a linear content-word reranker fitted on training lists
  (`final/content-fit.json` `1f4b0842…`, `final/content-lexicon.json` `1a65bf86…`); it is skipped when the top
  hypothesis leads by 3.75. The transformer's rotary-position tables are computed in double precision and rounded to
  f32, so this float stage gives the same words on every release platform (with f32 tables, macOS changed 3 of 730
  panel transcripts).
- **Live mode is unchanged:** v4.1.0's H247 weights, 160 ms chunks, beam 12, compact M5 live LM. `isosyll stream` keeps
  the v4.1.0 commit pass.
- **Commands and platforms are unchanged.** The model archive grows to about 720 MB unpacked (v4.1.0: 621 MB) for the
  final weights, reranker and lexicon.
- **Not in this release:** the CEO/CTO briefs (`docs/briefs`), which still describe v4.0.0.

## DEV accuracy (LibriSpeech DEV only; ultra-score, 10,000 speaker-cluster bootstrap replicates, seed 20261122)
All selection was on the DEV selection half (S, 2,782 utterances). The unselected half (C, 2,785 utterances) was decoded
once for the frozen finalist, then once more after the RoPE precision fix above (no setting changed).

| Split | v4.2.0 final pooled | dev-clean | dev-other | v4.1.0 final | v4.2.0 − v4.1.0 [one-sided 95% bounds] |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| panel (730) | 4.24% | 2.63% | 6.54% | 5.02% | −0.78 [−1.03, −0.54] pp |
| S half | 4.44% | 2.17% | 6.99% | 5.33% | −0.89 [−1.07, −0.71] pp |
| C half (unselected) | 4.20% | 2.52% | 5.90% | 4.87% | −0.67 [−0.79, −0.55] pp |

- **Full DEV (5,567 utterances; descriptive, contains S):** final 4.32% (2.34% clean, 6.42% other), 4,580 edits.
- **Against Zipformer INT8 (paired):** panel −1.01 [−1.35, −0.70] pp; C half −0.93 [−1.23, −0.66] pp (lower on both).
- **What the gate buys (S half, paired with the same pass without it, beam 16 + reranker):** −0.09 [−0.13, −0.04] pp.
- **Registered gates (from v3.1.0):** full-DEV final edits 4,580 ≤ 5,489, **pass**; final panel edits 613 ≤ 650,
  **pass** (v4.1.0: 726, miss). The live panel gate is still missed (734 against 722; live is unchanged).
- **RoPE fix:** the f64 tables changed 56 of 2,782 S-half transcripts (−10 edits), 12 of 730 panel transcripts (−4) and
  56 of 2,785 C-half transcripts (+10). Before the fix the release binary reproduced the research decode on all 2,782 S-half and
  730 panel utterances; the remaining differences are exactly the utterances whose reranker choice flips.

## Speed and energy (R9, quiet host H1, one pinned Zen 2 core)
R9 repeats the R8 energy protocol on the full 730-utterance panel: one pinned core of the quiet Zen 2 host (its SMT
sibling idle), 60 s idle windows and three counterbalanced blocks of five arms: v4.2.0 final, the same build with the
confidence gate off (a timing control, never shipped), the published v4.1.0 final, and the R8 anchors (v4.0.0 live +LM
default and Zipformer INT8). Pre-registration, receipts and the driver log are in
[`docs/paper/data/raw/release_v42/timing_interleaved/`](../../paper/data/raw/release_v42/timing_interleaved/README.md).

- **Timed build:** R9 timed the v4.2.0 release-candidate build `d0d03dc3`, before the RoPE precision fix. The published
  binary differs only in how the reranker's rotary-position tables are computed once at load; the per-utterance work is
  the same. Its R9 transcripts match the pre-fix scored transcripts on 730/730 panel utterances and the published
  binary's on 718/730.
- **Valid:** the block-1 anchors reproduced R6 within the pre-registered 5%: v4.0.0 live +LM 0.3336 J/audio-s (R6 0.328,
  +1.7%) and Zipformer INT8 0.5808 (R6 0.573, +1.4%).
- **Unclean blocks:** the host's four idle CI runner VMs stayed up throughout. v4.2.0 final and v4.1.0 final in
  block 1 and the live anchor in block 3 stayed core-unclean after their one rerun. They are kept in the receipts and left
  out of the energy and RSS medians and the paired energy ratios.

| Arm (730 utterances, 5,259.6 audio-s) | × real time (CPU) | Core J/audio-s | Peak RSS | Core-clean blocks |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| v4.2.0 final (gated beam 48 + reranker) | 14.8× | 0.721 | 451 MB | 2, 3 |
| v4.2.0 final, gate off (control) | 15.4× | 0.691 | 423 MB | 1, 2, 3 |
| v4.1.0 final | 16.3× | 0.662 | 387 MB | 2, 3 |
| v4.0.0 live +LM (anchor) | 31.0× | 0.331 | 110 MB | 1, 2 |
| Zipformer INT8 (anchor) | 13.6× | 0.585 | 150 MB | 1, 2, 3 |

- **Speed:** CPU-seconds per audio-second, median of the three blocks. **Energy:** idle-subtracted AMD core-energy
  registers (not package, board or wall power), median of core-clean blocks.
- **Cost of the new final pass (median of within-block ratios):** against v4.1.0 final, +10.3% CPU (blocks +10.6, +10.3,
  +9.1%) and +9.0% core energy, for −0.78 pp panel WER (−0.67 pp on the unselected C half).
- **The gate alone (against the gate-off control):** +5.0% CPU (blocks +5.9, +3.6, +5.0%; 4.99% unrounded) and +4.0% core
  energy. The budget set for the gate was +5% CPU. The owner locked the gate when research timing on another host put it
  just over (+5.8% and +5.9%). On R9 it lands at the budget: neither clearly inside nor clearly over.
- **Paired with Zipformer INT8 (blocks 2 and 3):** v4.2.0 final runs at 1.08× its throughput on 123% of its core energy.

## Install and run
```bash
curl -fsSL https://github.com/neverhuman/jsr/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh
isosyll transcribe meeting.wav            # final text (v4.2.0 final pass)
isosyll transcribe --mode live meeting.wav # 160 ms live text (unchanged)
```
Pin this release with `install.sh --version v4.2.0`; roll back with `--version v4.1.0`.
