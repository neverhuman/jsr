# Isosyll v4.1.0 (2026-11-27): lower live WER at the same power

**Status:** Released 2026-11-27 (MST) by owner decision. Exploratory DEV-only results; the registered edit gates below are stated as they fell.

## What changed
- **New weights, same architecture.** `weights.int8.safetensors` (60 MB, sha256 `c35b7c0a…`) is research run H247 at
  step 450: 450 steps of beam-12 minimum-word-error-rate fine-tuning of a weight average of three same-architecture research
  checkpoints (0.40 H175 + 0.30 H179 + 0.30 H241). `arch.json` (`a6a3eba6…`) describes the same layer shapes as v4.0.0;
  it is re-exported with H247's provenance.
- **Live decoder (the `--mode live` defaults):** 160 ms chunk, beam 12 (was 4), score margin 4.0, blank penalty 0.24,
  and a larger compact live LM: `lm/live.ulm` is `M5.v2.ulm` (243 MiB, sha256 `58ef726b…`; was `c4_p0_0_10_40.v2.ulm`),
  weight 0.70 with LODR 0.50 as before.
- **Final decoder (the `isosyll transcribe` default):** unchanged settings (commit chunk 16, beam 16, M5 LM at 0.80 with
  LODR 0.60, 320 ms tail pad); `lm/final.ulm` is `M5.ulm` (`72a621c2…`), as before. Only the weights change.
- **Commands, platforms and install are unchanged.** The model archive is larger (333.8 MB compressed, 621 MB unpacked; v4.0.0: 207.1 MB) because of the live LM.
- **Not in this release:** H244 and the terminal router (neither runs in the `isosyll` runtime, and their CPU cost was not
  measured), and the CEO/CTO briefs, which still describe v4.0.0.

once, for the frozen finalist.

  inconclusive); C half live +0.09 [−0.12, +0.30] pp (inconclusive) and final −0.26 [−0.55, −0.01] pp (lower).

## Speed and energy (R8, quiet host H1, one pinned Zen 2 core)
R8 repeats the R6/R7 energy protocol on the full 730-utterance panel: one pinned core of the quiet Zen 2 host (its SMT
sibling idle), 60 s idle windows, frozen 3bd85e9e decoders run with the v4.1.0 flags, and three counterbalanced blocks
of four arms (v4.1.0 live, v4.1.0 final, and the v4.0.0 live default and Zipformer INT8 as anchors). Pre-registration,
receipts and the driver log are in
[`docs/paper/data/raw/release_v41/timing_interleaved/`](../../paper/data/raw/release_v41/timing_interleaved/README.md).

- **Valid:** the block-1 anchors reproduced R6 within the pre-registered 5%: v4.0.0 live +LM 0.3247 J/audio-s (R6 0.328,
  −1.0%) and Zipformer INT8 0.5771 (R6 0.573, +0.7%).
- **Unclean block:** in block 3 both live arms stayed core-unclean after their one rerun (the measured core's SMT
  sibling was busy). They are kept in the receipts and left out of the energy and RSS medians and the paired ratios.

| Arm (730 utterances, 5,259.6 audio-s) | × real time (CPU) | Core J/audio-s | Peak RSS | Core-clean blocks |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| v4.1.0 live +LM (160 ms, beam 12, compact M5 LM) | 31.5× | 0.331 | 328 MB | 1, 2 |
| v4.1.0 final (commit pass, M5 LM, beam 16) | 17.0× | 0.656 | 387 MB | 1, 2, 3 |
| v4.0.0 live +LM (anchor) | 32.3× | 0.322 | 110 MB | 1, 2 |
| Zipformer INT8 (anchor) | 14.3× | 0.577 | 150 MB | 1, 2, 3 |

- **Speed:** CPU-seconds per audio-second, median of the three blocks (an unclean block can only be the slowest).
  **Energy:** idle-subtracted AMD core-energy registers (not package, board or wall power), median of core-clean blocks.
- **Paired with Zipformer INT8 (median of within-block ratios, blocks where both arms are core-clean):** v4.1.0 live runs at
  2.22× its throughput on 57% of its core energy; v4.1.0 final runs at 1.20× its throughput on 113% of its core energy.
- **Cost of the new live decoder (paired with the v4.0.0 live anchor, blocks 1 and 2):** +4.9% and +2.4% CPU, +1.4% and
  +4.2% core energy, for −0.46 pp panel WER (−0.71 pp on the unselected C half). Peak RSS rises from 110 MB to 328 MB
  (the 243 MiB live LM).
- **Not measured:** v4.1.0 final against v4.0.0 final in the same blocks (no RC2 final arm in R8), and package or wall power.

## Install and run
```bash
curl -fsSL https://github.com/neverhuman/jsr/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh
isosyll transcribe meeting.wav            # final text
isosyll transcribe --mode live meeting.wav # 160 ms live text
```
Pin this release with `install.sh --version v4.1.0`; roll back with `--version v4.0.0`.
