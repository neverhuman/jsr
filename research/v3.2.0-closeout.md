synthetic attenuation was 0.94 pp on DEV confirmation-half file-end commits
and 3.36 pp on DEV selection-half automatic-endpoint chapter streams. The

+0.31%. R10's 80 ms coalescing reduced whole-service CPU by 5.59% with transcript
parity and added 80 ms of lookahead and about 53 ms at p95 and p99. Core energy
was effectively flat and package energy remained unqualified. The coalescing
setting stays outside the release package. Fresh-capture qualification remains
pending an eligible replacement roster and a second domain.
